Company Profile

Growing better fruit. A brighter tomorrow.

JBA GreenGold Orchard grows premium mangoes and natural products in Ghana, working with the land and local communities for a brighter, more sustainable future.

Our story

JBA GreenGold Orchard was founded on a simple belief: great fruit can create better lives. Our work begins in the orchard, where patience, timing and stewardship shape the quality of every mango.

Our products

Premium fresh mangoes and natural mango products, including dried mango, juice, jam, pickles and gift collections.

Supply and partnerships

We work with local and international buyers. Our supply team can help with product availability, export requirements and delivery arrangements.

Our location

Ghana, with our contact team in Accra. Contact us to arrange a farm visit or discuss a partnership.
